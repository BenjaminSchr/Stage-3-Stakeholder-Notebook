# Flows

**Add a person**
When you enter a new person, then you fill in name, role, team, and what matters to them.
Note is optional. They then show up in the people list.

**Open the card before a meeting**
When you prepare for a meeting, then you search for the person and open their card.
You see role, what matters to them, recent conversations, and open commitments at a glance.

**Log conversation and commitments after a meeting**
When the meeting is over, then you log a conversation on the person card with date and note.
New commitments — yours or theirs — go in right alongside it, with a due date.

**Mark a commitment done**
When a commitment is fulfilled, then you mark it done on the person card.
It disappears from open commitments but stays visible in the history.

**Search**
When you remember a situation but not who it was with, then you search, for example "who was annoyed with onboarding?"
Search finds matching conversations and notes by meaning, not just by exact wording.

**Deactivate a person**
When someone is no longer relevant, then you deactivate the person instead of deleting them.
They disappear from the active list; conversations and commitments stay intact.
